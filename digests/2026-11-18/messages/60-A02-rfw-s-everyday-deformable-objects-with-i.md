**Summary:** RFW-S is a dataset of 83,365 simulation-verified deformable 3D assets across 485 everyday object categories and five structural families (soft solids, cloth sheets, shells, garments, bags), built with an automated reference-guided 3D generation and physics-modeling pipeline.
**Purpose:** The work targets the lack of scalable, physically grounded 3D asset construction and verification for deformable objects in existing simulation/robot-learning datasets.
**Breakthrough:** The authors report robotic grasping results (task success and object integrity) and sim-to-real replay of fragile-object manipulation using the dataset, positioning it as a scalable foundation for physically grounded simulation and robot learning.
**Tools & method:** The pipeline combines reference-guided 3D generation, structure-aware physical modeling, and LLM-assisted parameter estimation and calibration, followed by the independent verification framework described above.
