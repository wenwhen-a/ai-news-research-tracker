**Summary:** This is a survey/unifying-framework paper on 4D (dynamic) scene reconstruction, covering both implicit Neural Radiance Field (4D NeRF) and explicit 3D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) approaches.
**Purpose:** The paper aims to give the field a structured, representation-centric taxonomy connecting design choices (how a scene and its motion are represented) to measured outcomes (geometric fidelity, appearance consistency, efficiency), since prior methods' relationships were "fragmented." It is intended as a reference for understanding existing approaches and identifying open research directions rather than proposing a new method.
