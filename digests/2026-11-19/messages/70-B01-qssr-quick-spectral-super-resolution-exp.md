**[B01 · Update] QSSR (Quick Spectral Super Resolution) — expanded to Marvel's Spider-Man 2 and five more titles**
- **Company:** Sony Interactive Entertainment
- **Status:** GA · **Released:** 2026-11-13 · **Update**
- **Surface:** Base PS5 console feature, applied per-game via developer updates
- **Primary source:** <https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/qssr-arrives-in-marvels-spider-man-2-control-resonant-the-finals-arc-raiders-and-hell-let-loose-vietnam/>
- **Underlying research:** no traceable paper (QSSR is described as growing out of Sony/AMD's "Project Amethyst" collaboration; no arXiv paper cited)
- **Availability:** Free update, base PS5 (non-Pro); live now in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Fidelity and Performance modes); Control: Resonant update coming in December; The Finals and Arc Raiders "coming soon"; No Man's Sky "in the next few weeks"; Hell Let Loose: Vietnam via an "upcoming update."

**What shipped:** Update since 2026-10-02: QSSR, Sony's AI upscaler for the base (non-Pro) PS5 introduced October 1, 2026, is now live in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 and confirmed for five more titles (Control: Resonant, The Finals, Arc Raiders, No Man's Sky, Hell Let Loose: Vietnam) over the coming weeks/months.
**What research it translates:** The PlayStation Blog describes QSSR as sharing its underlying foundation and UI with PSSR (PS5 Pro's ML upscaler) and as an outgrowth of the ongoing Sony/AMD "Project Amethyst" collaboration.
**Practical significance:** Sony states QSSR delivers "sharper details, cleaner edges, and more stable images" versus traditional upscalers on base PS5 hardware.
**Engineering details:** No new technical detail (model size, inference cost, resolution targets) is disclosed beyond what the original October 1 announcement covered; this post is a developer-testimonial roundup, not a technical deep-dive.
