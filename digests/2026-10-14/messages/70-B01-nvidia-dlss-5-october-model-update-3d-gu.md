**[B01 · Update] NVIDIA DLSS 5 — October model update (3D-Guided Neural Rendering, 2X faster)**

- **Status:** GA · **Released:** 2026-10-13 · **Update**
- **Surface:** GeForce game-ready driver / NVIDIA App / RTX Remix — DLSS 5 Neural Rendering feature

The related Frame Generation "Preset C" is a separate opt-in NVIDIA App beta on RTX 40/50 Series and RTX Spark.

**What shipped:** Update since 2026-09-13 (NBA 2K27 DLSS 5 launch): NVIDIA shipped an "October update" to the DLSS 5 3D-Guided Neural Rendering model that it states is 2X faster than the September version, cuts the model to about 118 million parameters (a 24% reduction), and lowers 4K VRAM use by 20%, while claiming comparable visual fidelity and 10X the speed of the model NVIDIA first announced in March 2026. NVIDIA states the updated model raises frame rates in NBA 2K27 by up to 1.4X and will ship by default in future DLSS 5 titles.
**What research it translates:** NVIDIA describes DLSS Neural Rendering as a final AI pass that infers lighting and material detail from the game engine's rendered frame (geometry, textures, lighting buffers), building on the DLSS 5 architecture first shown in March 2026. The article does not cite a specific paper or technical report for this October model revision; this remains an internal, closed architecture.
