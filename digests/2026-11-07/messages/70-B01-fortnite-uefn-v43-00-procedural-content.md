**[B01] Fortnite / UEFN v43.00 — Procedural Content Generation, IK Retargeter, Gaussian-splat import**
- **Company:** Epic Games
- **Status:** GA · **Released:** 2026-11-01 · **New**
- **Surface:** shipped game / creator platform (Fortnite / Unreal Editor for Fortnite)
- **Primary source:** <https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/fortnite-ecosystem-v43-00/2848257>
- **Underlying research:** no traceable paper
- **Availability:** Live now for all UEFN creators, free. Gaussian-splat import and the Verse Health and Damage Pipeline are marked Experimental. PCG-graph creation/editing and the new animation/profiling toolsets are reachable by AI coding agents through UEFN's MCP server.

**What shipped:** Epic shipped Fortnite Ecosystem v43.00, adding Procedural Content Generation (PCG) to UEFN for automated large-world asset placement ("detailed worlds, like forests, cities, and custom points of interest"), an IK Retargeter that lets creators reuse existing animations — including on NPCs — on custom characters "with less manual setup," and Sequencer improvements for camera setup, character animation and cutscene editing.
**What research it translates:** The forum release notes name no underlying research paper for PCG, IK Retargeter or Gaussian-splat import — "no traceable paper."
**Practical significance:** A UEFN creator can populate large worlds automatically instead of hand-placing assets, retarget existing animation libraries onto new custom or NPC characters without re-rigging, and — per Epic — have an AI agent generate or edit PCG graphs via MCP, extending the agent-tool-calling pattern Epic introduced for UEFN earlier in 2026 into world-building and animation workflows.
**Engineering details:** Gaussian-splat import and the Verse Health and Damage Pipeline ship as Experimental; PCG and IK Retargeter ship as standard tools.
