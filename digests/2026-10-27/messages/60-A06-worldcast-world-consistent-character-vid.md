**Summary:** WorldCast is a framework for world-consistent character video generation under large-angle camera trajectories that combine rotation, translation, and radius change from arbitrary starting azimuths.
**Purpose:** The paper addresses the problem that existing camera-controlled video generation methods struggle with large-angle viewpoint changes that reveal character regions not visible in the first frame, causing geometric distortion, appearance degradation, and identity drift.
