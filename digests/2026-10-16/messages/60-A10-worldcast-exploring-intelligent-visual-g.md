**Summary:** WorldCast is a 320-case, 8-category benchmark for multi-step "world state prediction" — testing whether visual generative models can produce the correct sequence of future visual states given a scenario, beyond basic text-to-image/video generation.
**Purpose:** The authors argue that existing benchmarks mostly test simpler generation (static text-to-image, editing, basic video) and that the highest-level tasks — dynamic, physically plausible transitions guided by visual/physical/procedural references — are underexplored.
**Breakthrough:** The authors report evaluating 12 models and finding frontier proprietary image models advancing quickly (GPT-Image-2.5 scores 72.8 overall) while substantial gaps remain versus other systems (Nano Banana Pro 42.8, best open-source model SenseNova-U1 at 20.1; best video model Seedance 2.0 at 47.2).
