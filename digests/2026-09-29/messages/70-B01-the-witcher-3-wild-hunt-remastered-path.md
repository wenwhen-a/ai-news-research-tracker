**What shipped:** NVIDIA and CD PROJEKT RED shipped a free update adding full path tracing to The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered on PC, with DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction using NVIDIA's "second-generation transformer" AI denoising model.
**What research it translates:** This extends the same DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction transformer model and RTX path-tracing/hair-rendering pipeline NVIDIA has been rolling out across other titles this cycle (e.g. Control: Resonant, 007 First Light); no separate paper is cited on this page.
**Practical significance:** NVIDIA states performance reaches up to 385 FPS at 4K on RTX 5090 and 245 FPS at 4K on RTX 5080 in Ultra+ path tracing mode, making a 2015-era open-world game fully path-traced and AI-upscaled at high frame rates.
**Engineering details:** Ships alongside a new GeForce Game Ready Driver (released 2026-09-22, covering this title plus Control: Resonant, Gears of War: E-Day and AION 2).
