**Summary:** This is a technical report on LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6, an audio-driven avatar video generation model that builds on the team's prior 1.5 version, aiming to improve lip-audio synchronization, natural head/body motion, and visual consistency over long generated sequences.
**Purpose:** The authors note that prior audio-driven avatar generation struggles with precise lip-audio sync, natural motion, and visual consistency over long sequences, and that version 1.5 suffered issues like exaggerated lip movements and unstable, overly red lip coloration during extended generation.
**Breakthrough:** The authors report that arena evaluations show LongCat-Video-Avatar 1.6 outperforms its predecessor (1.5) and competing commercial audio-driven avatar models, and that the model can generate videos exceeding 10 minutes while maintaining visual quality and temporal consistency.
