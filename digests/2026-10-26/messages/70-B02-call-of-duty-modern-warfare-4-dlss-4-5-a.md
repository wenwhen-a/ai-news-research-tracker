**[B02] Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 — DLSS 4.5 at launch (plus Cyberpunk 2077 upgrade)**
- **Company:** NVIDIA (feature shipped in Activision's Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4)
- **Status:** GA · **Released:** 2026-10-23 (full launch) · **New**
- **Surface:** Shipped game (DLSS 4.5 feature)
- **Primary source:** <https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/dlss-4-5-cyberpunk-2077-planet-zoo-2-modern-warfare-4/>
- **Underlying research:** no traceable paper
- **Availability:** GeForce RTX 40-series+ gets Frame Generation; RTX 50-series adds Dynamic Multi Frame Generation (up to 6x); same wave also brings an Oct 22 Cyberpunk 2077 update moving its Multi Frame Generation/Ray Reconstruction/Super Resolution to DLSS 4.5's second-generation transformer models, and adds DLSS 4.5 to Planet Zoo 2.

**What shipped:** NVIDIA states Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 ships with the full DLSS 4.5 feature set — Super Resolution, Frame Generation and Dynamic Multi Frame Generation — timed to its October 23 full launch, alongside a Cyberpunk 2077 update upgrading its existing DLSS integrations to DLSS 4.5.
**What research it translates:** This is a continued rollout of the DLSS 4.5 Ray Reconstruction second-generation transformer model NVIDIA previewed in early access in August 2026; no new research paper accompanies this game-adoption announcement.
**Practical significance:** NVIDIA frames Dynamic Multi Frame Generation (RTX 50-series) as a frame-rate boost for one of the year's highest-profile shooters at launch, giving the DLSS 4.5 model its broadest AAA test yet.
**Engineering details:** The accompanying Game Ready Driver also enables Microsoft's Advanced Shader Delivery for Call of Duty and Gears of War: E-Day, precompiling shaders server-side to reduce local shader-compilation stutter.
