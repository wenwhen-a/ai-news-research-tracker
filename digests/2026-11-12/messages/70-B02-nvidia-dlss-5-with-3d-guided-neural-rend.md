**What shipped:** Update since 2026-09-13: NVIDIA states DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering is now live in War Thunder as part of the game's Eagle's Shadow update, moving War Thunder from the "confirmed for mid/late November rollout" status recorded on 2026-10-13 to an actually-shipped integration.
**What research it translates:** Same underlying DLSS 5 model already tracked under this entry (the "October 2026 Neural Rendering model," ~118M parameters).
**Practical significance:** NVIDIA states up to 400 fps at 4K and up to 550 fps at 1440p (Ultra preset, path tracing) in Gaijin's ground-battle benchmark, using Ray Reconstruction and up to 6X Multi Frame Generation.
**Engineering details:** Feature requires GeForce RTX 50 Series or RTX Spark hardware; NVIDIA's article does not state a specific required driver version for this update (a GeForce RTX/RTX Spark Game Ready Driver 621.24 WHQL was issued the same week for other titles).
**Limitation / caveats:** Where Winds Meet's DLSS 5 update, part of the same rollout plan, remains pending (NVIDIA states November 23) and has not shipped as of this check; it is not counted as a further update until it ships.
