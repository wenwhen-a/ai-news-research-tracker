## 调查：61% 欧洲游戏从业者担忧 AI 逼迫"更快产出"，仅 35% 担心被直接取代
* **日期：** 2026-09-08
* **来源链接：** https://www.gamesindustry.biz/european-game-developers-are-more-concerned-that-ai-will-pressure-them-to-work-faster-rather-than-take-their-job
