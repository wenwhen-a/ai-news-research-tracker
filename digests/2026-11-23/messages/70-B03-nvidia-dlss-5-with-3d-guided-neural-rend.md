**[B03 · Update] NVIDIA DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering (Where Winds Meet rollout)**
- **Company:** NVIDIA
- **Status:** GA · **Released:** 2026-11-23 (Where Winds Meet rollout) · **Update**
- **Surface:** Shipped games (PC/GeForce NOW, in-game "Neural Rendering" toggle)
- **Primary source:** <https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/news/hogwarts-legacy-war-thunder-dlss-5-3d-guided-neural-rendering/>
- **Underlying research:** NVIDIA Research project page, "DLSS 5: Generative Neural Rendering" — describes a real-time, one-step pixel-space diffusion model; no arXiv preprint found.
- **Availability:** GeForce RTX 50 Series desktop/laptop GPUs, RTX Spark systems, and GeForce NOW Ultimate; free, no waitlist, via a Game Ready Driver.

**What shipped:** Update since 2026-09-13: NVIDIA and developer Everstone Studio/publisher NetEase added DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering to the wuxia open-world game Where Winds Meet, available November 23 in North America (November 24 elsewhere), following the same feature's earlier November rollouts to Hogwarts Legacy (Nov 5) and War Thunder (Nov 12).
**What research it translates:** DLSS 5 is built on a generative, diffusion-based neural renderer that treats the game engine's finished frame — geometry, textures, lighting buffers and motion vectors — as a conditioning signal for AI-generated final pixels, per NVIDIA's own research description.
**Practical significance:** NVIDIA states the feature now covers four shipped titles (NBA 2K27, Hogwarts Legacy, War Thunder, Where Winds Meet) plus RTX Remix, giving RTX 50-series/RTX Spark/GeForce NOW Ultimate owners a toggleable, no-cost visual upgrade.
**Engineering details:** Delivered via a Game Ready Driver update tied to each game's own patch, rather than a separate downloadable model; NVIDIA iterated the underlying model several times this quarter ahead of this rollout.
