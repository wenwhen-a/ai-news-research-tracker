Its "hits" are LEADS ONLY: keep ids whose first four digits fall in the window months, drop obviously off-topic titles (medical, physics, NLP, recommender systems, GUI agents), and remember that "Google" on abstract-only pages usually comes from the "Google Scholar" link and "Meta" often matches "meta-learning".

No PDF fallback: if there is no HTML version, record the paper as a near-miss "affiliation unverifiable".
